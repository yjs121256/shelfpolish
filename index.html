How it works

1. Send your export

In Shopify admin, export your products as CSV and email it to us, plus any facts you want used (materials, sizes, care) and words you never use.

2. We rewrite and check

AI drafts the copy from your data. Automated checks then flag any number that is not in your source data, a list of strong-claim words (such as "organic" or "waterproof") that your data does not support, and any SEO field over its length limit.

3. You import

You get import_ready.csv, changelog.csv, flags.csv and a 1-page guide. Only five columns change; everything else is copied as-is. Test one product, then import the rest.
